Student Management Sequence

Follow the sections in this order to complete the student management workflow.

1. Institution Setup

Register or edit institution details, admin access, branch, phone, and available sections.

2. Student Information

Add student identity, family, contact, academic, group, and section information.

3. Attendance

Record daily present or absent status and notify registered numbers by SMS.

4. Marks

Enter subject marks, calculate totals and rankings, and send marks updates.

5. Fees

Enter net fees and the current balance for each student.

6. Progress

Review marks totals, percentages, and individual student progress.

7. Events

Record institution events and prepare notifications for students.

8. Review & Export

Compare overall performance, then download Excel files or PDF reports.
